Customer Churn Predictor

Chosen model — recall

80%

Three models compared on 7,032 telecom customers: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Decision Tree. Random Forest won on raw accuracy (79%), but Logistic Regression was chosen instead for its 80% recall — because missing a customer who's about to churn costs more than a false alarm. Feature importance confirmed contract length and tenure as the strongest churn signals.
